Model assumptions, sources and credits

Circular orbits at fixed representative distances. Planets, including Pluto, share one plane; Earth’s tilt is 23.44° and the Moon’s orbital tilt is 5.145°. Only Earth’s Moon is included. Model days are not calendar predictions. Local solar time changes the observing longitude; sunrise/noon/sunset are separate longitudes on the model Earth at 45° N. Daily sweeps hold the orbital arrangement fixed.

No eccentricity, precession, refraction, terrain, light travel time, stellar background, rings or other moons. Daylight color is illustrative, not a visibility calculation. Phases use a point Sun; hard shadows can darken the Moon. Surface colors are illustrative. Pluto’s real inclined, eccentric orbit is deliberately simplified.

Sources: JPL planetary elements, JPL sizes and periods, NASA Pluto, Moon, Sun, and lunar tilt.
